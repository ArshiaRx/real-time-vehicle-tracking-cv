\vspace{0.1cm}
\noindent\textbf{2. Introduction: Problem Statement and Chosen Algorithms}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{Problem Statement:} Accurate vehicle counting in traffic videos faces challenges: occlusions, similar appearances, varying lighting, and real-time requirements. We combine classical CV (optical flow) with deep learning (YOLOv8) for robust tracking.

\vspace{0.05cm}
\noindent\textbf{Chosen Algorithms and Models:}
\vspace{-0.1cm}
\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{|p{4cm}|p{8.5cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Lucas-Kanade Optical Flow} & Sparse feature tracking: $\nabla I \cdot \mathbf{v} + I_t = 0$ \\
\hline
\textbf{YOLOv8 Detection} & Single-stage deep learning detector for real-time vehicle detection \\
\hline
\textbf{Kalman Filter} & State estimation ($\mathbf{x} = [x, y, v_x, v_y]^T$) for occlusion handling \\
\hline
\textbf{Hungarian Algorithm} & Optimal data association using IoU + appearance features \\
\hline
\textbf{Perspective Transform} & Homography matrix for bird's-eye view transformation \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent\textbf{4. Experimental Methodology}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\noindent\textbf{Datasets:} Custom traffic video dataset (2+ videos) with varying densities, lighting, camera angles. Manual annotation for ground truth.

\noindent\textbf{Evaluation Metrics:} Counting accuracy ($\frac{\text{True Count}}{\text{Ground Truth}} \times 100\%$), tracking precision (MOTA), FPS, false positive rate.

\noindent\textbf{Experimental Setup:} Python 3.8+, OpenCV 4.8+, Ultralytics YOLO, Streamlit. Hardware: CPU with multi-core support (recommended) or GPU with CUDA support for faster YOLO inference. Baseline: Optical Flow vs. YOLOv8. Parameters: confidence (0.3--0.7), min box size (20--50px).
